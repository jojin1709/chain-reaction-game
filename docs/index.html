How it Works

A round alternates step-by-step between writing and drawing!

STEP 1

✍️ Write Prompt

Everyone writes a secret phrase to kick off their chain.

STEP 2

🎨 Draw Picture

You draw the prompt received from the previous player.

STEP 3

🤔 Guess Image

You guess what the previous player's doodle represents.

STEP 4

🎉 Full Reveal

Watch every chain unfold from prompt to final guess!
